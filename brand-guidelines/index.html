Logo system

Our logo system contains five marks. Nothing is a font: every lockup is outlined vector art and must be placed, never rebuilt.

MH Full Stack
The proper treatment of the name and the default in almost every application.
M Mark
May be used prominently or as an accent/background element.
Triangle Mark
Occasionally prominent, more often a background element.
Triangle Groups
Background or small accent only. Never stands in for the logo.
MH Full Horizontal
Restricted. Only use with express permission.

Scaling Elements

The proportions of the primary logo's two elements are fixed and should only be scaled together and proportionally.

Similarly, the proportions of the triangles in the Triangle Group mark are fixed and should not be changed nor should any the individual triangles be removed or adjusted.

Reversed logos, for light on dark

There is a separate white/light artwork set. It exists because the diagonal slash in the M reads too small when the dark artwork is simply flipped to white, so the reversed files are drawn for the job rather than derived from the black ones.

What' the difference?

Right
Cut widened and spacing corrected.
Wrong
A hairline through the M.
Right
The cut is opened and reads properly at any size.
Wrong
The cut appears too thin.
Do

Use the drawn white files whenever the mark sits on a dark ground. A cream tint is made by recoloring the reversed artwork, never the black.

Don't

Never reverse a black logo to make a white one. The result is not the white logo.

The tracked eyebrow

All caps, Apparat SemiCondensed, tracked between +100 and +250, bronze on light or brass on dark. Interpuncts separate the parts, not hyphens and not slashes.

The statistic row

Serif numerals over tracked-caps labels, evenly spaced, sitting between two hairlines. Numerals use tabular figures so columns line up. Labels are bronze or brass; values take the ground's text color.

Voice and copy

Our name

Full name on first reference and in any legal or contractual context: Mont Haus International Realty. After that, Mont Haus alone.

Two words, both capitalized, no hyphen, no internal capital. Not MontHaus, not Mont-Haus, not MHIR, not Mont Haus Realty, and not MONT HAUS in running copy (the all-caps form belongs to the logo artwork only).
