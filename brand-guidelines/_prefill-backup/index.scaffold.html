This page is the single reference for how the Mont Haus identity is used. It covers the logo system, colour, type, imagery and the way we write, and it is written to be handed to anyone producing work in our name, inside the firm or outside it. Everything shown here is downloadable at the bottom of the page.

Logo system

Five marks are live. Anything else found in an older folder is retired and should not be used.

MH Full Horizontal
Restricted. See below.

When each one is used

  • MH Full Stack is the proper treatment of the name and the default in almost every application.
  • MH Full Horizontal is only for placements that require a long, narrow lockup, such as a Vail Daily classified ad. Outside that constraint it is not a proper treatment of the name, so reach for the stack instead.
  • The M Mark can carry a piece on its own and may be used at any prominence.
  • The Triangle Mark (the large single triangle) can occasionally be used prominently, but its usual job is as a background element.
  • Triangle Groups (the multi-triangle artwork) is a background texture or a small accent. It is never a prominent mark in place of the logo.

Reversed logos, for light on dark

There is a separate white artwork set. It exists because the diagonal slash in the M reads too small when the dark artwork is simply flipped to white, so the reversed files are drawn for the job rather than derived from the black ones.

Do

Use the drawn white files whenever the mark sits on a dark ground.

Never

Reverse a black logo to make a white one. The result is not the white logo.

Not available

There is no white horizontal lockup, and the horizontal should not be used on dark at all.

Never do this

Each of these has a right version. Pair every item with a wrong and right image once the examples are shot.

  • Do not reverse the black logo to make a white one. Use the drawn white file.
  • Do not use the horizontal lockup where the stack fits.
  • Do not use the horizontal lockup on a dark ground.
  • Do not use Triangle Groups in place of the logo.
  • Do not pull artwork from a retired folder. Five directories are live: MH Full Stack, M Mark, Triangle Mark, Triangle Groups, MH Full Horizontal.
  • TO SUPPLY: stretching, recolouring, outlining, adding effects, rebuilding the lockup, setting the name in another face.

Downloads

Put files in assets/pdf/ and assets/logos/. Use .pdf, .zip, .svg, .png, .jpg, .eps or .ai. The server blocks several extensions site-wide, so a download saved as .conf, .sql, .env, .old, .bak, .orig, .log, .lock, .py, .sh or .example will 403 with nothing on screen explaining why.
